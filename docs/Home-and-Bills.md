# Home & Bills

Electricity, water, rent, insurance, the car tax: when each is due, what you paid, and where the paperwork is.

## Add a bill

On the right: **Bill**, the **usual amount** (optional; an estimate is fine), how often it **repeats** (every month, every 3 months, every year, or once), the **next due** date, and a note such as the account number or "blue folder".

## Due

Sorted by due date, each row says "due in 2 days", "due today" or "overdue by 3 days" (accented when it is near or late) and shows the amount, how often it repeats and the note.

- **Paid…** asks for the amount actually paid (filled with the usual one); **Confirm** records it and moves the bill to its next date, keeping the day of the month. `Esc` cancels.
- With [Expenses](Expenses.md) on, the payment is also recorded there under **Bills** with the bill's name.
- A **one-off** bill leaves Due when paid but stays in history.
- A notification appears **three days before** a bill is due and again when it is **overdue**, once each.

## Paid

The **Paid** tab lists every payment, newest first. The heading shows how much you paid this year.

Removing a bill deletes its payment history too (it asks first).

Key: `N` adds a bill. Home shows the next bill (or how many are overdue); [Weekly Review](Weekly-Review.md) lists what you paid and what is due.

Tip: for fixed charges you do not need to confirm each time (streaming, phone), use [Subscriptions](Subscriptions.md).
