# Settings

How Vermil looks. Open it with **Settings** in the top bar or `Ctrl+,` (press again, `Esc` or **Back** to return). Changes apply at once and are remembered.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: Auto (follows Windows), Light or Dark. The button in the top bar does the same.
- **Accent colour**: ten ready colours or your own. The accent marks the one thing that matters in a view, so Vermil keeps it readable: a colour that is too pale or too dark on light or dark paper is moved to the nearest one that is, and it says so. Text on the accent and small accent text are recomputed to stay legible in both themes.
- **Font**: Anuphan (bundled, the default), Windows (Segoe UI), Serif, Mono, or **Other**: type the name of any font installed on your computer. Thai text always falls back to Anuphan, so it never turns into boxes. If the name is not installed, Vermil keeps Anuphan.
- **Size**: 90%, 100%, 110% or 125% for everything (like the browser zoom, kept by Vermil and applied at start). Wide sizes keep the fixed layout; 125% on a small screen may switch to the one-column layout.
- **Motion**: follow Windows, or switch animations off.
- **Top ticker**: show or hide the line of words under the top bar.
- **Reset the look** puts the colour and font back.

The look is stored on this computer and applies to the Today widget too. It is not part of a backup or sync.

## General

- **Start with Windows** and the **Morning summary** (a notification at a time you choose, once a day) are here, no longer in the footer.
- **Week starts on** Monday or Sunday: the calendar, the week strips in Health and Shopping and the Weekly Review follow it.
- **Clock**: 24-hour or 12-hour.
- **Open on**: Home, or the tab you were on when you closed Vermil.
- **New task reminder**: the reminder a new task starts with.
- **Tab order**: move the tabs with the arrows. `Ctrl+1`, `Ctrl+2`... follow the order you set, and the More menu does too.

Week start and clock are read while drawing, so changing them reloads the window once and returns here.

## Notifications

- **Quiet hours**: nothing is shown between two times (a range may run over midnight, such as 22:00 to 07:00). A task reminder that comes due then is shown when the quiet hours end.
- **Notify me about**: switch off the notifications of one source: task reminders, the focus timer, medicine, bills, subscriptions, birthdays, countdown, budget warnings (only the modules you have on are listed). The morning summary has its own switch above.
- **Send a test notification** shows one regardless of quiet hours.

## Shortcuts

The three that work from any app: **Quick add a task** (`Ctrl+Alt+T`), **Quick note** (`Ctrl+Alt+N`) and the **Today widget** (`Ctrl+Alt+W`). Press a button, then the new keys: Ctrl, Alt or Shift with one key. `Esc` cancels. If another program already uses the combination, or another Vermil shortcut does, you are told and the old one stays. **Back to the usual keys** resets all three. The shortcut list (`?`) and its warning follow your choice. The keys inside the quick bar (`Ctrl+Alt+J`, `Ctrl+Alt+N`) do not change.

## Data

- **Backup** and **Sync between computers** (they were on Home): see [Home, backup and sync](Home.md).
- Where your data file is and how big it is, with **Open the data folder**.
- **What each module has saved**: the size of each module's data, and **Delete** to empty it (it asks and says it cannot be undone; Tasks and Notes are not in this list). A module that is on starts again empty; one that is off stays off.
