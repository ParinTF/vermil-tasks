# People

Birthdays, gift ideas and a nudge when it has been too long since you were in touch.

## Add or edit a person

On the right: **Name**, **Birthday** (the year is only used to show an age; tick **Show their age** to see "turns 36"), **Stay in touch** (never, or every week, 2 weeks, month, 2 months, 3 months), a **Note** and **Gift ideas**, one per line. Adding someone counts as being in touch today. **Edit** on a row loads it into the form; **Cancel** goes back to adding.

## The list

Sorted by the next birthday (people without one last). Each row shows the birthday and how far away it is (accented within a week), when you were last in touch, the note and the gift ideas.

Tabs: **Everyone**, **Birthdays soon** (within 30 days) and **Reach out** (people whose interval has passed). Row actions:

- **In touch today** resets the clock.
- **Plan a catch-up** adds a task "Catch up with Mali" three days from now at 18:00 (when [Tasks](Tasks.md) is on).
- **✕** removes a person (it asks).

A notification appears **on the day** and **three days before** each birthday, once each.

Key: `N` adds a person. Home shows the next birthday; [Weekly Review](Weekly-Review.md) lists birthdays in the next 14 days and who to reach out to.
