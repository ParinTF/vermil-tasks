# Health

A simple log for you: water, sleep, weight, workouts and medicine. It is not medical advice.

Use `‹` `›` (or the arrow keys) to look at or fix another day; `Today` (or `T`) comes back.

## Cards

- **Water**: glasses of about 250 ml against your target (8 to start). `+` and `−` change it; `W` adds a glass from the keyboard; the palette has "Drink a glass of water".
- **Sleep**: type the hours slept the night before (`7.5` or `7,5`) and press `Tab` or `Enter`. Clearing the box clears the day.
- **Weight**: type today's kg. A small chart shows the last 30 days and how much it moved.
- **Workouts**: pick a kind (Walk, Run, Gym, Yoga, Cycle, Swim, Other), the minutes, **Add**. The card shows this week's total; ✕ removes one.
- **Medicine**: add a name, the times (`08:00, 20:00`) and the days. Today's doses are a checklist; tick each one when you take it. At each time a **notification** appears once if the dose is not ticked (Vermil must be running, which it is in the tray).

Targets (water glasses, sleep hours) are in the right column.

Works with: Home tile (water and doses left), [Goals](Goals.md) (workout minutes, average water and sleep), [Weekly Review](Weekly-Review.md).
