# Expenses

Record what you spend and earn, by category, month by month. Adds a **Money** tab.

## Add an entry

On the right: choose **Spent** or **Income**, type the **Amount** (`1,250.50` works), pick a **Category**, the date and an optional note, then **Add**.

- **One-tap categories**: Food, Transport, Bills, Shopping, Health, Fun, Invest, Savings, Other for spending; Salary, Dividends, Gift, Refund, Other for income. Categories you used before join them, most used first. A chip fills the field and jumps to the amount.
- A minus sign is not accepted as an amount (use Spent or Income).
- **Currency symbol** (right column) is `฿` to start; change it any time. Other modules use the same symbol.

## The month

Use `‹` `›` (or the arrow keys, `T` for this month) to change month.

- **Spent, Income, Balance** at the top (added in whole cents, so `0.1 + 0.2` is `0.3`).
- **Spending by category**: bars for the six biggest.
- **The list** of the month's entries; **✕** on a row deletes it (it asks first).

## Budgets

Under the currency, **Monthly budget**: type a category and a limit, press **Set**. A **Budgets** block shows spent of the limit with a bar. From 80% it says "almost"; from 100% it turns accent and says by how much it is over. You also get a notification the first time a category reaches 80% and again at 100% in a month. Income and other months do not count. Setting the same category again changes the limit; **✕** removes it.

## Keys

`N` new entry, `←` `→` month, `T` this month.

Works with: [Shopping & Meals](Shopping-and-Meals.md) ("Log to Expenses") and [Home & Bills](Home-and-Bills.md) ("Paid…") record entries here; [Goals](Goals.md) can watch money saved or spent; Home shows spending today.
