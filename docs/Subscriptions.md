# Subscriptions

Everything that renews, with the cost per month and a reminder before each charge.

## Add one

On the right: **Name** (Netflix), **Price**, **Every** week, month or year, and **Next charge**. A charge on the 31st comes back on the 31st after a short month.

## The list

Sorted by the next charge, with "in 3 days", "tomorrow", "today". Within 3 days it turns accent. For yearly and weekly prices the monthly cost is shown too. At the top: **Per month**, **Per year** and the count.

- **Paid** means the charge happened: the next one moves a cycle on (and never lands in the past).
- **✕** removes one (it asks first).
- A notification appears **3 days before** each charge, once.

The currency symbol is the one you set in [Expenses](Expenses.md) when that module is on, otherwise `฿`.

Key: `N` new. Home shows the next renewal and the monthly total; [Weekly Review](Weekly-Review.md) lists what renews in the coming week.

Tip: for bills whose amount changes each month (electricity, water) use [Home & Bills](Home-and-Bills.md).
